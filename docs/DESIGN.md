# HanziGo visual direction

HanziGo uses a restrained editorial palette of charcoal, Chinese-inspired red, ivory and soft green. Prominent Chinese text, a compact seal-inspired 汉 mark, generous spacing, rounded cards and subtle shadows make the learning content the focal point. The dark lesson card gives the home screen a stronger visual anchor; the three daily metrics are arranged together on small phones.

The interface responds to phone and wider layouts, supports light/dark/system themes and mirrors navigation and text direction for Arabic. Decorative artwork is hidden from accessibility readers. Interactive controls use semantic roles and minimum 44-point targets. The design still needs real-device and assistive-technology verification; see `QA.md`.
